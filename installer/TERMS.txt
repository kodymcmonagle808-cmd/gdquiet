2. HOW GD QUIET WORKS
GD Quiet runs locally on your Windows PC and reads only the configured
screen region (or Geometry Dash window percentage) using Windows OCR to:
  - Mute or unmute the background Windows apps you explicitly choose.
  - Optionally send your configured Discord "Toggle Deafen" hotkey.
All percentage detection, audio control, and configuration happen locally
on your device. No gameplay footage or personal audio is uploaded.
